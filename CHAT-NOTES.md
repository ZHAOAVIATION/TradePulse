# TradePulse

TradePulse is Aaron Cornites’ responsive multi-market dashboard. Visitors enter the public charting dashboard; the owner can sign in through Supabase. The app shows TradingView charts and technical summaries for indices, forex, commodities, stocks, and crypto. It does not place trades or guarantee results.
